Privacy notice

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This notice explains what personal data SOLVAI collects, how it is used, who can see it, and the choices you have.

1. Who is responsible

Acies Cusp Services LLP ("CUSP"), Unit No. 3, Christella, 206 Whitefield Main Road, Near Hope Farm Junction, Whitefield, Bengaluru 560066, decides how and why your personal data is used in SOLVAI. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, CUSP is the data fiduciary.

Voyce, operated by Rishav Raj, Bengaluru, India, builds and runs SOLVAI for CUSP. Voyce is CUSP's data processor: it handles your data only on CUSP's instructions and only to provide SOLVAI.

2. What we collect

  • Account: your name and email address, from signing in with a one-time code sent to your email. Sign-in is handled by Clerk.
  • Your setup: your name, role line, topics, tone, posting time and time zone, and the facts and writing rules you or CUSP add.
  • Voice samples: past posts you paste and files you upload so SOLVAI can learn how you write, and the style summary made from them.
  • LinkedIn connection: the access token LinkedIn issues when you connect, your LinkedIn member ID and name, and when the connection expires.
  • Content: your drafts, edits, approvals and skips, the posts you publish, and the news articles each draft was based on.
  • Write history and post ideas: what you write and make in SOLVAI, kept so new ideas don't repeat a recent post.
  • Consent record: when you agreed, at LinkedIn connect, to CUSP seeing your drafts and posts.
  • Technical data: IP address and request details, used for security and to prevent abuse, and error logs.

SOLVAI takes no payments, and it uses no analytics or advertising trackers.

3. How we use it

  • To draft content in your voice and, when you approve it, publish it to your LinkedIn account.
  • To send service emails: a draft is ready, a reminder from CUSP, your LinkedIn connection is about to expire, your monthly drafts are used up.
  • So CUSP's team can set up your voice and support you (section 4).
  • To keep the service secure and enforce seat limits, and to answer your support requests.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Who can see it

  • You.
  • CUSP's team: your drafts, posts, setup, approvals and skips, and whether your LinkedIn is connected. Not your LinkedIn password, and nothing else from your LinkedIn account. CUSP cannot approve or publish for you.
  • Voyce: as CUSP's processor, to run SOLVAI and to fix problems you or CUSP report.
  • Not other SOLVAI users. Clients never see each other's data.

6. AI-written content

To write a draft, SOLVAI sends the relevant text to Anthropic's Claude API, and to Google's Gemini API for AI pictures, YouTube videos and as a backup when Claude is unavailable. Each processes it under its own API terms. AI-written content can contain mistakes, so please read each draft before you approve it.

7. Where your data is processed

Some of the providers above store or process data outside India, including in Singapore and the United States. We use only providers that protect data under their own security and privacy commitments.

8. Cookies and local storage

  • Sign-in cookies from Clerk keep you signed in. They are needed for SOLVAI to work.
  • A short-lived cookie (li_oauth) is set while you connect LinkedIn, so the connection can only finish in the browser that started it. It lasts 10 minutes.
  • Local storage in your browser keeps drafts and app preferences on your device.

SOLVAI uses no analytics or advertising cookies.

9. How long we keep it

  • Account, setup and content: while your seat is active. After your seat ends, until you or CUSP ask for deletion; we then delete it within 30 days.
  • Drafts waiting for approval: until the next day's draft replaces them, and 48 hours at most.
  • Write history: 90 days, then deleted automatically. Post ideas: 14 days.
  • LinkedIn connection: until you disconnect, it expires (about 60 days), or your seat ends, whichever comes first.

10. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, withdraw consent you gave, or nominate someone to exercise these rights for you. Write to CUSP's grievance officer, MB Sam (Founder & CEO), at sam@cusp.services. We reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with the answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Withdrawing consent to CUSP seeing your drafts means your seat ends, because that review is part of the service CUSP provides.

11. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Your LinkedIn access token is used only to publish what you approve and to show your LinkedIn name and photo on your drafts. Access to data is limited to the people and providers who need it to run SOLVAI.

12. Children

SOLVAI is not for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

13. Changes

If we change this notice in a way that matters, we will tell you by email or in SOLVAI before the change applies.
