Questions

Before you talk to CUSP.

Can anything go out without my say-so?

No. Every draft waits for you. Nothing reaches LinkedIn until you tap Approve, and a skipped day stays skipped.

Where do the facts come from?

From what you tell SOLVAI, the facts you agree with CUSP at setup, and this week’s news in your field. Each morning draft shows the articles it drew on, and SOLVAI is told never to invent numbers, clients or quotes. You read every draft before anything goes out.

What can SOLVAI do on my LinkedIn?

You connect once, through LinkedIn’s own sign-in. SOLVAI can then publish the posts you approve, under your name, and show your name and photo on your drafts. It never sees your password and can’t read your messages or connections. You can disconnect at any time in LinkedIn’s settings.

Who sees my notes and drafts?

Only you and CUSP’s team, who use them to set up your voice and keep you on track. Only you can approve or publish, and other clients never see yours.

How is my data protected?

It travels over encrypted connections and is stored encrypted. To write a draft, SOLVAI sends the text it needs to its AI providers, Anthropic and Google. The privacy notice lists every provider. Ask CUSP, and your data is deleted.

What can I give SOLVAI?

A rough thought, a YouTube link or notes you’ve written. Or nothing at all: each morning it drafts a post from this week’s news in your field.

How is it priced?

Per seat, one for each person who posts. Start with one leader and add more when you’re ready. CUSP takes you through it on a short call. Use the form at the foot of this page.

Can I stop?

Yes, any time. Tell CUSP and your seat ends. What you’ve posted stays on your LinkedIn.
