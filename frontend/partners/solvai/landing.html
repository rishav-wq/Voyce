Questions

What people ask first.

Does SOLVAI post without me?

No. Every draft waits for you. Nothing reaches LinkedIn until you tap Approve, and you can edit or skip any draft first.

Who sees my drafts?

You and CUSP’s team. CUSP sees your drafts and posts so it can set up your voice and keep you on track, but only you can approve or publish. Other clients never see yours.

Is my LinkedIn account safe?

You connect through LinkedIn’s own sign-in page, so SOLVAI never sees your password. You can disconnect at any time, and you can always copy a draft and post it yourself instead.

What does it cost?

Your company buys seats through CUSP, one per person who posts. Each seat includes 60 drafts and 10 AI pictures a month. Talk to CUSP for a price for your team.

How do I start?

Join the waiting list at the foot of this page. After a short call, CUSP sets up your voice and sends you an invite. Your first drafts arrive within the week.

Can I stop?

Yes, any time. Tell CUSP and your seat ends. What you’ve posted stays on your LinkedIn, and you can ask for everything else to be deleted.
