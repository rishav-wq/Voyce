Questions

What people ask first.

Does SOLVAI post without me?

No. Every draft waits for you. Nothing reaches LinkedIn until you tap Approve, and you can edit or skip any draft first.

How much of my time does it take?

Setup is one conversation with CUSP. After that, reading a draft and approving or skipping it takes a few minutes. Skip any day you’re busy.

Is what I share kept confidential?

Only you and CUSP’s team see your notes, drafts and posts. CUSP sees them so it can set up your voice and keep you on track. Only you can approve or publish, and other clients never see yours.

To write a draft, SOLVAI sends the text it needs to its AI providers, Anthropic and Google. The privacy notice lists every provider.

SOLVAI is told not to invent numbers or stories, and you read every draft before it goes out.

Can my assistant or team approve posts for me?

No. Posts go out from your own LinkedIn account, so only you can approve them. Your team can send ideas to CUSP to shape your drafts.

I’m in the US. Does it work for me?

Yes. Your draft arrives in the morning in your own time zone.

Is my LinkedIn account safe?

You connect through LinkedIn’s own sign-in page, so SOLVAI never sees your password. You can disconnect at any time, and you can always copy a draft and post it yourself instead.

What does it cost?

Your company buys seats through CUSP, one per person who posts. Each seat includes 60 drafts and 10 AI pictures a month. Talk to CUSP for a price for your team.

How do I start?

Use the form at the foot of this page. Sam from CUSP books a short call, sets up your voice and sends your invite. Morning drafts start the day after you sign in.

Can I stop?

Yes, any time. Tell CUSP and your seat ends. What you’ve posted stays on your LinkedIn, and you can ask for everything else to be deleted.
