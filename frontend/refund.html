Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

There's nothing to cancel

Voyce Pro is a one-time payment for a 30-day pass. It never renews or charges you again, so there is no subscription to cancel — when the pass ends, your account simply returns to the free plan unless you choose to buy another.

7-day full refund

If Voyce isn't right for you, email hello@voyce.co.in within 7 days of your payment and we'll refund it in full — no questions asked. Please write from the address you signed up with, and include your Razorpay payment ID if you have it. This refund is available once per account.

How refunds are paid

  • We issue the refund through Razorpay to the card, UPI or bank account you paid with, within 2 business days of your request.
  • Banks usually take 5–7 business days to show it. For international cards it can take longer, and the amount you see may differ slightly because of exchange rates and your bank's fees.
  • Pro access ends when the refund is issued.

After 7 days

Passes are not refundable after 7 days. If Voyce fails to provide the service because of a problem on our side — for example, a long outage — tell us and we'll extend your pass or refund the unused days. Charges made in error, such as a duplicate payment, are always refunded in full.

Nothing in this policy limits any rights you have under Indian consumer protection law.
