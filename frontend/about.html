About Voyce

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Voyce is an AI LinkedIn ghostwriter for consultants, coaches and fractional executives — people who win clients on LinkedIn but don't have time to write every day.

You tell Voyce what you post about and show it a few of your past posts. Each day it finds news in your niche, writes your take in your voice, and has the post ready at the time you choose. By default it emails you each post to approve with one tap; if you'd rather not check, it can post for you. It connects through LinkedIn's official API and never sends DMs or connection requests.
