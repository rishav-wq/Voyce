Your best salesperson is often your worst sales coach.

They close because of instinct they can't explain. Promote them to manager and the team gets stories, not a method.

Three things that work better:
1. Coach from recorded calls, not memories.
2. Write down the questions your top closer asks, in order.
3. Measure the manager on the team's win rate, not their own deals.

Which of these does your team skip?
