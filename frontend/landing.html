Questions

Before you try it.

What is Voyce?

Voyce is an AI LinkedIn ghostwriter for consultants, coaches, and fractional executives. It finds this week's news in your niche, writes your take in a voice learned from your past posts, and has it ready each morning for you to read and approve with one tap. It connects through LinkedIn's official API.

Will the posts actually sound like me?

Yes — that's the core of it. Add a few of your past LinkedIn posts and Voyce writes in your rhythm, vocabulary, and tone, so it reads like you wrote it, not generic AI.

Do I have to approve every post?

Yes, by default: Voyce emails you each post with the news it is based on, and nothing goes out until you tap Approve, from your phone, with no login. Skip a post and nothing happens; the next morning's post replaces it, so old news never goes out. A post that fails Voyce's fact check is always held for you. Automatic posting exists as a setting, and it is off unless you turn it on.

Won't LinkedIn ban my account?

Voyce connects through LinkedIn's official API, the same kind of access scheduling tools use. It never asks for your password, never uses a browser extension, never scrapes LinkedIn, and never sends DMs, connection requests or pod activity. No tool can promise how LinkedIn treats an account, but Voyce stays inside the official integration, and by default you approve every post.

What do I get for free?

5 free tries: each post, carousel, image card, preview or list of post ideas uses one, and one of them can be an AI picture. You can post them to LinkedIn yourself. No credit card required.

Is my content private?

Your profile data and posts are yours, and Voyce never sells them. To write your posts, Voyce sends the relevant text to its AI providers: Anthropic (Claude) writes and checks them, and Google Gemini draws AI pictures and reads YouTube videos. The Privacy Policy has the details.

Can I cancel anytime?

There's nothing to cancel — Pro is a 30-day pass with no auto-charge. You renew only when you want to.

How much does Voyce cost?

Free for 5 posts, with no card. Pro is a 30-day pass: ₹1,999 in India and $49 (billed as ₹4,699) elsewhere. It never renews by itself, so there is nothing to cancel.

What is an AI LinkedIn ghostwriter?

Software that writes LinkedIn posts for you in your own voice, the way a human ghostwriter would. A human LinkedIn ghostwriter typically charges $1,000–$5,000 a month and needs a weekly call. Voyce learns your style from your past posts, finds this week's news in your field, and has a post ready each morning for you to approve.

Does it work for a company page too?

Not yet; company pages need a separate LinkedIn approval. On Pro, each of your 3 profiles can post to a different personal LinkedIn account: invite a client and, once they connect their LinkedIn, that profile publishes as them.
