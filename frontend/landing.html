FAQ

Questions? Answered.

What is Voyce?

Voyce is an AI LinkedIn ghostwriter for consultants, coaches, and fractional executives. It finds today's news in your niche, writes your take in a voice learned from your past posts, and publishes to LinkedIn on your schedule — through the official LinkedIn API, so your account stays safe.

Will the posts actually sound like me?

Yes — that's the core of it. Upload your past LinkedIn posts and Voyce learns your rhythm, vocabulary, and tone, so what it writes reads like you wrote it, not generic AI.

Does it post automatically, or do I approve first?

Your choice. Turn on "ask me before it posts" and Voyce writes on your schedule but holds each post for your one-tap approval. When you trust it, flip to full autopilot — every post logged with the news source it reacted to, and one click pauses everything.

Won't LinkedIn ban my account?

No — Voyce posts through LinkedIn's official OAuth API, the same door Buffer and Hootsuite walk through. No browser bots, no cookies, no scraping, and never any auto-DMs, auto-connects, or pods.

What do I get for free?

5 generations to try — text posts, carousels, and image posts — plus manual posting to LinkedIn. No credit card required.

Is my content private?

Yes. Your profile data and posts are yours. Voyce only uses them to generate your content.

Can I cancel anytime?

There's nothing to cancel — Pro is a 30-day pass with no auto-charge. You renew only when you want to.

Does it work for a company page too?

Yes. Create separate company and personal profiles, each with its own style, schedule, and topics.
