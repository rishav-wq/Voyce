KnowErgo (by Knowella) — AI video ergonomics. Tagline: "Catch strain before it becomes injury." Upload a short worker video; it scores musculoskeletal-injury risk from the footage. No wearables.

WHAT IT DOES (all real, from the product):
- Scores a task on 6 recognised systems in one pass: REBA, RULA, NIOSH lifting equation, Snook/Liberty Mutual, HSE MAC, WISHA. Lead with RULA/REBA/NIOSH; the other three are depth.
- 133-keypoint pose model -> 36-joint skeleton -> 10 body angles (neck, trunk, both arms, both wrists, both knees), scored EVERY SECOND, left and right side independently.
- Per-body-part risk breakdown: % of the clip each joint spent low/medium/high, plus avg and peak angle.
- Before/after: re-assess the same task after a change and prove the risk dropped (improved/worsened per joint).
- Multi-person tracking; privacy face/background blur available.
- Refuses to score low-confidence frames (draws a grey "?" rather than guessing) — honesty is a feature.

RED-FLAG THRESHOLDS (cite these, they're real): neck >20deg, trunk >60deg, upper arm >40deg, knee <120deg. Per-part medium/high: Neck 20/45, Trunk 20/60, Shoulder 45/90, Elbow 60/100, Wrist 15/30, Knee 30/60. NIOSH Lifting Index >3 = redesign required. Snook <75% of the working population capable = intervention needed.

HSE MAC risk bands (from the HSE Manual Handling Assessment Charts): G low, A medium, R high (prompt action), P very high. Manual Handling Operations Regulations 1992 hierarchy: avoid -> assess -> reduce. MSDs are the most common occupational ill health in the UK.

WHO IT'S FOR: EHS/safety managers, ergonomists, plant/ops leaders in manufacturing, warehousing, logistics, construction. Outcome language: "fewer sprains and strains, fewer recordable injuries", "assess every task, not just the sampled few", "the same frameworks ergonomists use".

NEVER SAY (hard rules):
- Never say it "replaces your ergonomist" — it's the assessor's instrument. Lead with "score every task", not "fire the expert".
- Never state an injury-cost or prevalence statistic that isn't from a cited source or the HSE facts above. No round numbers from memory.
